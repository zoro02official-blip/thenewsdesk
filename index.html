About
Independent. Rigorous. Trusted.
The News Desk was founded on a simple belief: that quality journalism — deeply researched, honestly reported, and free from commercial influence — is essential to a functioning democracy.

Our Mission

We exist to hold power accountable and give voice to those who are too often unheard. Every story we publish is thoroughly researched, verified against primary sources, and reviewed for accuracy before it reaches your screen.

Our newsroom operates independently of advertising influence. We are funded entirely by our readers, ensuring that our coverage remains objective and in the public interest.

Independent Reporting
Our coverage is never influenced by advertisers, political parties, or commercial interests. The newsroom operates with complete autonomy.
Rigorous Fact-Checking
Every claim is verified against multiple independent sources. We correct errors promptly and transparently, with full editorial notes.
Reader-Supported
Over 200,000 paying subscribers fund our journalism. This model keeps us accountable to readers — not to corporate shareholders.
Global Reach
Our correspondents report from 48 countries, giving us an on-the-ground perspective that wire services and aggregators simply cannot replicate.

Privacy Policy
Last updated: January 2025
1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you subscribe to our newsletter, create an account, or contact us. This includes name, email address, and communication preferences. We also collect usage data including pages visited, articles read, and time spent on our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to deliver our newsletter, personalize your reading experience, improve our journalism, and communicate with you about our services. We never sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting privacy@thenewsdesk.space.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site functionality may be affected.
